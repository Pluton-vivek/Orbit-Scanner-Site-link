3. Description of the App

Orbit Scanner is a mobile document intelligence application for Android that allows you to:

  • Scan documents using your device camera with automatic edge detection.
  • Organize scanned files and folders on your device.
  • Use PDF tools: Merge, Compress, Protect, Image-to-PDF, PDF-to-Image, and PDF-to-Word.
  • Use AI and OCR tools: Extract Text, Layout OCR, Translate, Enhance, and Extract Tables.
  • Sign documents electronically using the eSign feature, with signatures stored locally on your device.
  • Share, export, and print documents.
