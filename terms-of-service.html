3. Description of the App

Orbit Scanner is a mobile document intelligence application that allows you to:

  • Scan documents using your device camera with automatic edge detection.
  • Organize scanned files and folders on your device.
  • Use PDF tools: Merge, Compress, Protect, Image-to-PDF, PDF-to-Image, and PDF-to-Word.
  • Use AI and OCR tools: Extract Text, Layout OCR, Translate, Enhance, and Extract Tables.
  • Sign documents electronically using the eSign feature.
  • Share, export, and print documents.

4. Free and Pro Plans

4.1 Free Plan

The Free plan provides access to core features with usage limits:

  • Unlimited scanning and local storage.
  • PDF tools: limited to 3 operations per day, with watermarked outputs.
  • OCR (Extract Text): 5 operations per month.
  • AI tools (Layout OCR, To Word, Translate, Extract Tables): 3 operations per month combined.
  • eSign: 1 document per month.
  • No ads. The Free plan is and will remain ad-free.

4.2 Pro Plan

The Pro plan removes all usage limits and adds premium features:

  • Unlimited PDF tool operations without watermarks.
  • Unlimited AI/OCR operations.
  • Unlimited eSign.
  • Advanced accuracy modes.
  • Cloud sync across devices.
  • Priority support.

Pro is available as a Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly subscription.
