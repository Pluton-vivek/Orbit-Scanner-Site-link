🚀Getting Started

What is Orbit Scanner?

Orbit Scanner is a mobile document intelligence app for Android. It combines several tools that usually require separate apps: document scanning, PDF tools, AI-powered OCR, document translation, and electronic signatures — all in one focused experience.

It’s built privacy-first: no ads, no data selling, no AI training on your documents.

How do I get started after installing?

Open the app, tap through the brief onboarding, and you’re ready to scan. Tap the scan button (the FAB at the bottom right of the Home screen) to capture your first document. You don’t need to create an account to start scanning — an account is only required for cloud-based features like AI/OCR and eSign.

Do I need an account to use the app?

You can scan and manage documents locally without an account. An account is needed for:

  • AI/OCR features (Extract Text, Layout OCR, Translate, To Word, Enhance)
  • eSign
  • Managing your usage credits

Sign in with Google for the fastest setup — no password to remember.

Is Orbit Scanner free?

Yes. Orbit Scanner is free to use, with no paid plans, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Scanning and local storage are unlimited. AI/OCR features use a usage-credit system (see the Credits section). There are no ads.

Which languages does the app support?

The app interface is available in English and Hindi. OCR works with many languages including English, Hindi, and other Indic scripts (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi), as well as European, East Asian, and Arabic scripts. Translation supports many language pairs.

📸Scanning

How does auto edge detection work?

When you point your camera at a document, the app uses on-device processing to detect the document edges in real time. You’ll see an overlay around your document. When the document is steady and well-framed, the app auto-captures and applies perspective correction so the output is rectangular.

For best results: ensure the document is on a contrasting surface (e.g. white paper on a dark table), and use good lighting.

Can I scan multiple pages at once?

Yes. After capturing the first page, tap Add Page to capture more. You can scan an entire booklet or contract in one session, then review, reorder, or remove pages before saving as a single PDF.

Edge detection isn’t working. What should I do?

A few quick fixes:

  • Improve the contrast between the document and the surface beneath it.
  • Move to a better-lit area or turn on the flash.
  • Hold the phone parallel to the document (not tilted).
  • If auto-detection still fails, switch to manual capture and adjust the crop corners yourself on the review screen.
Can I import images instead of scanning?

Yes. On the scan screen, tap the gallery icon to import images already on your device. Edge detection runs on imported images too.

What filters are available?

You can apply these filters after capture: Original (no enhancement), Auto (recommended for most documents), Black & White (for high-contrast text), and Magic Color (best for printed color documents). Try each one in the preview — switching is instant.

Does scanning work offline?

Yes, scanning, cropping, filtering, and saving all work fully offline. You only need an internet connection for cloud-based features: AI/OCR and translation.

🤖OCR & AI

What’s the difference between Extract Text and Layout OCR?

Extract Text pulls plain text from your scan — great for simple documents like notes or letters where formatting doesn’t matter.

Layout OCR preserves the original structure: headings stay as headings, tables stay as tables, columns stay as columns. Use this when you need a clean, formatted output that mirrors the original document.

How accurate is the OCR?

OCR and AI results are produced by automated systems and are not guaranteed to be 100% accurate. Accuracy depends on scan quality, lighting, and document complexity. Clear, well-scanned documents produce the best results; handwriting, faded text, or low-resolution scans will produce lower accuracy. Always verify extracted text, translations, and converted documents against the original, especially for important documents.

Does OCR work with Hindi and other Indic languages?

Yes. We’ve invested specifically in Devanagari accuracy because most scanner apps treat Hindi as an afterthought. Our OCR handles Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, and other Indic scripts. Mixed Hindi+English documents are also supported.

Does OCR work offline?

Basic OCR (Extract Text) may run on-device depending on your phone. Layout OCR and other AI-powered features require an internet connection because they use our cloud AI provider. You’ll see a clear message if a feature requires internet.

What happens to my document when I use AI features?

When you tap an AI feature, your document is securely sent to our processing servers, processed by our AI provider, and the result is returned to your phone. The document is then deleted from our servers immediately — typically within 60 seconds. We do not keep copies, cache it, or use it for AI training. Read more in our Data Protection document.

📄PDF Tools

What PDF tools are available?

The PDF toolkit includes:

  • Merge PDF — combine multiple PDFs into one
  • Compress PDF — reduce file size
  • Protect PDF — add password protection
  • Image to PDF — convert one or more images into a PDF
  • PDF to Image — export each PDF page as an image
  • PDF to Word — convert a PDF into an editable Word document
Can I password-protect a PDF I’ve already scanned?

Yes. Open the file, tap Tools → Protect PDF, set a password, and save. You’ll need that password to open the protected PDF in any reader. We can’t recover lost passwords, so write it down safely.

How good is PDF-to-Word conversion?

Conversion works well for text-heavy PDFs with simple layouts. Complex layouts, tables, and embedded images may need some manual cleanup in Word. The output is a true .docx file that you can edit directly. As with all AI/OCR features, review the output against the original.

🌐Translate

How many languages are supported for translation?

Many languages, including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Mandarin Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, and more. The full list is available in the Translate screen when you select a target language.

What are the three output formats for translation?
  • Translated Image (Preview) — the translated text is overlaid on the original document layout, so it looks like the original but in another language. Best for visual fidelity.
  • Side by Side — original and translated text shown together. Best for comparison.
  • Text Only — just the translated text, no layout. Best for copying/sharing translated content.
Can I improve translation quality?

Yes. In the Translate config screen, toggle Improve accuracy ON. This uses a higher-quality translation model and takes slightly longer to process. Recommended for important documents. Note that no translation is guaranteed to be perfectly accurate — always review the result.

Is translation free?

Yes. Translation is free and uses your usage credits like other AI features (see the Credits section).

✍️eSign

How do I sign a PDF?

Open the PDF, tap Sign, then either draw a signature with your finger, upload an existing signature image, or type a signature. Tap on the PDF where you want the signature to appear, then resize and position it. You can also add text fields, dates, and stamps. Save the signed PDF when done.

Are Orbit Scanner signatures legally binding?

No. The eSign feature is for convenience and informal use. It does not meet the standards of legally binding electronic signature laws like the Indian IT Act, the US ESIGN Act, or the EU eIDAS Regulation. We do not provide identity verification, audit trails, tamper-evident sealing, or certificate-based signatures.

For legally binding signatures (employment contracts, real estate, legal agreements), use a certified eSign service.

Where are my signatures stored?

Signatures are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to our servers. You can delete saved signatures anytime from the Sign menu.

🎫Credits

What are usage credits?

AI- and OCR-powered features (Layout OCR, Translate, To Word, Enhance, Extract Tables) use a usage-credit system to keep the service fair for everyone. Your account is allotted a number of credits, and each eligible operation uses some of them. You can see your allotted, used, and remaining credits in the app.

Do credits cost money?

No. Orbit Scanner is free, and credits cannot be bought, sold, or exchanged for cash. They simply manage fair usage of the AI/OCR features. We do not collect any payment, card, bank, or UPI information.

What happens when I run out of credits?

When your available credits are used up, the AI/OCR features may be temporarily unavailable until your credits refresh according to the current allocation. Scanning, local file management, and on-device processing are not limited by credits.

A feature failed but a credit was still used. What do I do?

If a cloud AI operation fails due to a network or server error, the credit should not be deducted. If you see a credit was incorrectly used, email support@plutontechnology.com with the date and time, and we’ll look into restoring it.

🔒Privacy

No. Documents you send to the cloud for AI/OCR processing are deleted from our servers within 60 seconds of processing completing. We do not retain copies, archive them, or use them for anything other than fulfilling your immediate request. Do you train AI on my documents?

No. We do not train AI models on your data. Our AI/OCR provider is contractually prohibited from using your data for model training. Read more in our Data Protection document.

Do you show ads?

No. Orbit Scanner has no ads anywhere, and we do not collect data for advertising purposes.

Do you sell my data?

No. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Is my data encrypted?

Can I request a copy of the data you store about me?

Yes. Registered users can request their stored account data (account creation date, credit/usage information, and any retained document/processing data) by emailing support@plutontechnology.com from the email address registered with the account. See Privacy Policy, Section 7 for details.

👤Account

How do I change my email or name?

Go to Profile → Profile in the app. If you signed in with Google, your name and email come from your Google account — update them there.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete Account (at the bottom). Review the information shown and confirm your request.

Your account is then marked for deletion and enters a 30-day recovery period. If you log back in during those 30 days, your account is restored automatically and the deletion is cancelled. If you don’t, your account and associated personal data are permanently deleted at the end of the period. Documents stored locally on your device are not affected. See Privacy Policy, Section 8 for full details.

I changed my mind after requesting deletion. Can I recover my account?

Yes. During the 30-day recovery period, simply log back into the app with your account. Your account will be restored automatically and the deletion request cancelled. After the 30-day period ends, permanent deletion cannot be reversed.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Email support@plutontechnology.com from your registered email address and ask for a copy of your account data. We respond within 30 days (or sooner where required by law). See Privacy Policy, Section 7.

🔧Troubleshooting

The app keeps crashing. What should I do?

Try these in order:

  • Force-close the app and reopen it.
  • Restart your phone.
  • Make sure you have the latest version from Google Play.
  • Free up some device storage (the app needs space for scans).
  • If it still crashes, email support@plutontechnology.com with your phone model, Android version, and a description of what you were doing when it crashed.
OCR is producing garbled output. Why?

OCR accuracy depends heavily on scan quality. Try:

  • Rescanning with better lighting
  • Holding the phone steadier (use a tripod or rest on a flat surface)
  • Using the Auto filter before running OCR
  • For Hindi or other Indic documents, make sure the document language is detected correctly
  • Try Layout OCR with “Improve accuracy” enabled for complex documents
Why does the app need camera and storage permissions?
  • Camera — required to scan documents.
  • Storage — required to save scans to your device and to access files you want to import (e.g. importing a PDF to merge).

We do not access your storage in the background. We only read files you explicitly select.

Which Android versions are supported?

Orbit Scanner supports Android 8.0 (Oreo) and later. We test on a range of devices from Redmi, Realme, Samsung, Motorola, OnePlus, and Pixel.
