Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your Orbit Scanner account and associated data — online, without needing the app.

Orbit Scanner is operated by PLUTON TECHNOLOGIES (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED. You can delete your account directly inside the app, or submit a request using the form below and our team will process it.

Fastest way: delete in the app

If you can sign in to the app, this is the quickest method:

  1. Open Orbit Scanner and sign in with your registered account.
  2. Go to Profile → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom, review the details, and confirm.

What happens after you request deletion

Your account enters a 30-day recovery period. If you log back into the app within 30 days, your account is restored automatically and the request is cancelled. If you do not, your account and associated personal data are permanently deleted at the end of the period.

What is deleted:

  • Your account and profile information (name, email).
  • Your credit and usage data.
  • Any account-associated data retained on our systems, except where the law requires us to keep it.

What is not affected: documents saved locally on your device (you manage those yourself), and documents sent for AI/OCR processing are already deleted from our servers within ~60 seconds of processing. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
