Why we built Orbit Scanner

The mobile document scanner category has stagnated for nearly a decade. Most apps you’ve tried likely felt the same: cluttered with ads, gated behind dark-pattern subscriptions, and vague about what happens to the documents you scan. We knew we could do better.

Orbit Scanner started with a simple question: what would a document scanner look like today if you started from scratch, removed every dark pattern, and treated every user with respect?

The answer is this app.

What Orbit Scanner does

Tools that typically require several separate apps, combined into one focused experience:

  • Smart Document Scanning. Auto edge detection, perspective correction, multi-page batch scanning, and enhancement filters that make your scans look sharp.
  • Complete PDF Toolkit. Merge, compress, password-protect, convert between images and PDFs, and export to editable Word documents.
  • AI-Powered OCR. Extract text from any scanned document or image. Layout OCR preserves headings, tables, and columns — not just a wall of text.
  • Document Translation. Translate scanned documents into many languages with three output formats: translated image, side-by-side, or text-only.
  • eSign. Sign PDFs directly on your phone. Draw signatures, upload images, add date stamps, and place text — with signatures stored locally on your device.
